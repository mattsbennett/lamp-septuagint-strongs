## What that does not cover

Two things in this repository came from other people and keep their own terms. Both are permissive;
[`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) has the details.

- **The Strong's numbers and dictionary forms in the tagged text** are STEPBible's, under
  CC BY 4.0. If you republish the tagged text, credit STEPBible.org and Tyndale House, Cambridge,
  and link to their data. That is the one obligation that travels with the data.
- **`data/source/versemap_lxx_to_english.json`** is worked out from SIL's versification tables,
  under the MIT licence. Keep SIL's notice with that file if you redistribute it. It is an input to
  the build and no part of the tagged text.

Brenton's Greek text itself (`data/source/LXX.json`, and the `surface` column of every table) is in
the public domain.
